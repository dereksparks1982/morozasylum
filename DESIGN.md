# Moroz Asylum | Setting and game design

This file records decisions from the design conversation. Status: **unvalidated foundation candidate**.

## Confirmed direction
- Title: Moroz Asylum. In-world Russian name: Лечебница «Мороз» (Lechebnitsa Moroz).
- Cherny Bor, Чёрный Бор, literally Black Pinewood or Black Forest, is a fictional **small town**, not an island.
- A compact, clustered industrial town: railway, steelworks, quarry/logging, residential quarters, civic services, the asylum and a separate forced-labor installation. Surrounding pine forests, mountains and cold climate.
- Medford, Oklahoma is the intended **scale comparison** for the town plus industrial area, not an architectural model. US Census Bureau: 932 residents in 2020; 1.1 square miles. Cherny Bor's fictional population remains undecided.
- Civilians, institutions, town gossip, families, local businesses, friendships, politics and off-duty pursuits matter, just like off-factory life in Federal Electric.
- Historical flavor, Russian vocabulary and clickable educational links, with distinctions between documented events, rumor and fictional drama.
- Ordinary asylum-management opening. Secret state assignments enter as later scripted developments. Also random events, persistent characters and player choices.
- Potential historical seasons under the tsars, early USSR, Stalin and later USSR. **No initial year chosen yet.** Use era-appropriate architecture: later Soviet prefab apartment blocks should not appear in an 1800s campaign.

## Foundation scaffolding
A self-contained GitHub Pages browser game shell, inspired by Federal Electric's interaction pattern. Office and interactive portrait placeholder; departmental tabs; patient and staff tables; compact town locations; journal; manually advanced days; schematic chart; save to browser local storage; scripted and random correspondence. Game economy and numbers are placeholders. Town geometry, maps and visuals are not final.
